[Description] (4000 characters)
A private chat for two, with a tiny Buddy you raise together.

puny-chat is a one-on-one chat app for you and one friend. Above your conversation is a little room, home to a Buddy you both look after. The more you talk, the sooner the egg hatches and the more your Buddy grows.

■ Chat like you always do
・Invite one friend with a 4-character code or a link
・See when they're typing, online, or have read your message
・Send photos, one at a time
・Get a notification when a new message arrives

■ Care for it as you chat
・Feed your Buddy when it's hungry, and clean up when it poops 💩
・Or just send "feed" or "clean" in the chat
・Tap your Buddy and it smiles, dances or sings
・When you both send a message on the same day, you get a together bonus
・Your Buddy never dies. Miss a few days, and a little care is all it takes

■ Watch your Buddy and its room grow
・It grows from egg to baby, kid and adult, and starts talking as a kid
・At Lv.12 it finds a job based on what you two do: chef, gardener, radio DJ, writer or barista
・Every level brings new furniture and things to the room
・The light changes from morning to evening to night, and seasonal touches drop by
・Tap ♨ and your Buddy unwinds in the hot spring
・Raise it to Lv.60 and it can go its own way. Its photo stays in your memory album, and a new egg arrives

■ Easy to start
・No account needed. Use it as a guest for 30 days, or link your Apple or Google account to keep going
・No ads, no purchases, no quests. Just talk
・Available in English, Japanese and Korean
